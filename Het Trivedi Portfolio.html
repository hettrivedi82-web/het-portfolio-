Process

From raw clips to ready to post

  1. Brief

    You send the raw clips, the reference style and where the video will be posted.

  2. Rough cut

    I trim, order and pace the story so the hook and flow are right before any effects.

  3. Polish

    Sound, captions, transitions and colour go in. This is where the edit starts to hit.

  4. Delivery

    You get the final export in the right size, and I fix any small changes you ask for.
